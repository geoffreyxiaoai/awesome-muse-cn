# Contributing

Thanks for helping curate the Muse ecosystem! This project is bilingual (English/中文). You may contribute in either language — the maintainer will add the translation.

## How to suggest a resource

- **Easiest:** open an issue with the "Suggest a resource" template.
- **Or:** fork, add your entry in the right section of `README.md` (English), and open a PR. The maintainer will mirror it to `README.zh-CN.md`.

## Entry format

```markdown
- [owner/repo](https://github.com/owner/repo) — One honest line: what it does, who it's for, any caveat. ⭐123 · note
```

Rules for the one-liner:

1. **Must be about Muse** — Meta's personal AI assistant (muse.ai). Not Muse Code, Muse Spark/Glimmer models, or other same-named projects.
2. **Say something real.** "A collection of Muse skills" is not a review. What does it do, and what's the catch?
3. **Flag risks honestly.** Mark ToS-risky, reverse-engineered, or unverified entries with ⚠️ and a short note.
4. **One line.** Keep it tight; link out for details.
5. **Star counts** are refreshed by the weekly maintenance sweep — don't worry about exact numbers in your PR.

## Review process

- A maintainer bot scans GitHub weekly for new Muse-related projects, refreshes star counts, checks dead links, and flags archived repos. It prepares a draft update.
- Every change (bot drafts and human PRs alike) is reviewed by the maintainer before merge.
- Entries that go stale, break, or turn out to be unrelated get moved out or removed — curation means pruning too.

## Code of conduct

Be kind, be honest, no spam. Promotional entries for your own project are fine as long as the one-liner is truthful about what it does.
